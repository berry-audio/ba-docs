# Radio

Berry Audio includes a built-in Internet Radio player. Enjoy high-resolution playback from a wide variety of stations available worldwide.

> **Note:** Internet Radio requires an internet connection.

### Features

- Curated list of 200+ radio stations
- Genres include Pop, Rock, 80s, News and more
- Major stations such as BBC, FluxFM and SomaFM

## Radio Browser

You can also search and play stations from the [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/) directory using the search feature:

1. Click the **search** icon at the top of the screen.
2. Enter the name of the station.
3. Play the station, add it to your **Favourites**, or add it to a **playlist**.
   > **Note:** A dedicated Radio Browser module is currently in development.
