# Radio Browser

A dedicated Radio Browser extension is currently in development. In the meantime, you can find stations using search:

1. Click the **search** icon at the top of the screen.
2. Enter the name of the station. Results are fetched from [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/).
3. Play a station, add it to your **Favourites**, or add it to a **playlist**.
